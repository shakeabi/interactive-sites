Unbounded behaviour, bounded consequences

You cannot make a language model behave predictably. That is not a temporary limitation, and no prompt closes the gap. So the engineering discipline of agentic systems is almost entirely about the second half: given an actor whose next move you cannot predict, what does the surrounding system guarantee anyway? Every stage below pairs a behaviour you can't control with the invariant a competent system enforces regardless.
