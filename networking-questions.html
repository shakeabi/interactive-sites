Reading the candidate

competent

Knows the mechanisms when named. Needs the layer pointed out, then reasons correctly within it. Fixes the symptom that was described.

strong

Asks for the distribution, the counters, the RTT before theorising. Computes rather than recalls. Notices when two stages are one bug. States what a fix costs.

exceptional

Rejects a plausible fix with a reason. Says “the network was fine, the process was paused.” Arrives at idempotency and fencing without being led. Knows which guarantees are impossible and stops trying to buy them.

Run one problem per 45 minutes. If a candidate solves a stage instantly, skip ahead — the depth badges let you jump straight to the STAFF stages. If they stall, hand them the follow-up as a hint rather than the answer; how someone uses a hint is more informative than whether they needed one.
